# Anonymized Data Terms

The anonymized incident data distributed with SOCpilot may be used and
redistributed for research and reproducibility, subject to both conditions:

1. Preserve the anonymization when using or redistributing the data.
2. Do not attempt to reidentify individuals or the originating institution.

These terms apply to the distributed anonymized incident records and copies or
transformations of those records. They do not grant access to raw operational
exports, private mappings, credentials, or institutional systems. Include these
terms when redistributing the data so recipients know the applicable conditions.

The source code is separately licensed under [MIT](LICENSE). The data terms
are purpose-restricted; they are not an unrestricted open-data license. Model
weights, third-party datasets, and dependencies retain their respective terms.
