01 / Principle

Generation is probabilistic.
Policy checks are reproducible.

SOCpilot maps a proposed response to a finite action catalog and checks three operational rule families. The verifier records each violation and repair without asking another model to infer compliance from prose.

02 / Method

One fixed protocol.
Every decision auditable.

  1. IncidentNormalized, anonymized package.
  2. ActionsFinite catalog of response actions.
  3. PolicyReviewed predicates with explicit scope.
  4. ProtocolFixed matrix, hashes, ledger, and denominators.
  5. ReplayAnalysis reproducible without rerunning the model.
